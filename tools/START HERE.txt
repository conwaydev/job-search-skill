JOB SEARCH: START HERE

You need the Claude desktop app (claude.ai/download) on a paid plan.

1. Move this JobSearch folder somewhere you'll keep it, like your home folder
   or Documents. Not the Downloads folder, and not inside a synced folder
   like iCloud Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox.

2. Open the Claude app and click the Code tab. Choose "Open folder" (or the
   folder picker) and pick this JobSearch folder. If it asks whether you
   trust the folder, say yes.

3. Type:  set me up

That's it. Claude checks your computer, installs what it needs inside this
folder, and walks you through the rest, one question at a time. It takes about
20 minutes, and you can stop whenever you like. Next time, open the same
folder and say hi; it picks up where you left off.

Everything stays on your computer, in this folder. Nothing is sent anywhere
except the searches you ask for. Don't delete this folder: it is your search.
