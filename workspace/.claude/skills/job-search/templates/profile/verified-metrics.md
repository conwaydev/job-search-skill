# Verified facts and numbers

Every number or specific claim that can appear in a resume, letter or application comes from this file. Nothing marked `unverified` or `do-not-use` is ever written into anything an employer reads.

Each entry:

    ### The claim, in plain words
    Status: confirmed | unverified | do-not-use
    Use: anywhere | applications-only | interview-only
    Source: where it comes from (report, dashboard, review, manager, own estimate)
    Notes: context, rounding rules, anything to be careful about
